About

The work

She understands the strategic complexities, cultural diversity and value of brands across global, regional and local structures, and implements a unified voice across all communication platforms.

The values

Ruchi is a staunch supporter of DEI values. She has studied Human Rights and co-created the first digitally-led global campaign on Human Rights for the UNHRC.
